## Voice

Energetic, playful and company-led — funny, casual, respectful and enthusiastic in tone.

- **Energetic:** Upbeat, high-intensity language (0.9 superlatives per 100 words). _e.g. “We use a combination of research-backed teaching methods and delightful content to create courses that effectively teach reading, writing, listening, and speaking skills!”_
- **Playful:** Uses humour, exclamations or emoji to keep things light. _e.g. “Learning with Duolingo is fun, and research shows that it works!”_
- **Company-led:** Speaks as "we" — 68% of sentences are about the company. _e.g. “We use a combination of research-backed teaching methods and delightful content to create courses that effectively teach reading, writing, listening, and speaking skills!”_
- **Purpose-driven:** Frames the brand around a bigger cause or community. _e.g. “They are core beliefs that support our promise of high-quality education for all and nurture the environment of innovation, excellence, and lifelong learning that we are quite proud of!”_
- **Action-oriented:** 27% of headlines open with a verb. _e.g. “stay motivated”_

**Do**
- Keep headlines to about 3 words (site median).
- Write headlines in sentence case.
- Lead headlines with a strong verb (Build, Get, Start…).
- Speak as "we" when describing what the company does.
- Use contractions (you're, it's) to sound human.
- Aim for a grade 10 reading level (Flesch 55).

**Don't**
- Don't end headlines with a full stop.
- Avoid technical jargon; keep language plain.
- Don't use emoji in brand copy.
