## Voice

Action-oriented, confident & understated and technical — serious, formal, respectful and matter-of-fact in tone.

- **Action-oriented:** 37% of headlines open with a verb. _e.g. “Get to market faster.”_
- **Confident & understated:** Makes claims plainly: no exclamation marks, few superlatives. _e.g. “Fast, if you seek it out.”_
- **Technical:** Comfortable with specialist vocabulary (2.6 technical terms per 100 words). _e.g. “Run your business on a reliable platform that adapts to your needs.”_
- **Polished & formal:** Measured, complete sentences at a grade-9.9 reading level. _e.g. “Grow your business with a comprehensive set of payments and financial tools—designed to work individually or together.”_
- **Concise:** Short sentences averaging 13.7 words. _e.g. “Fast, if you seek it out.”_

**Do**
- Keep headlines to about 5 words (site median).
- Write headlines in sentence case.
- End headlines with a full stop — 43% of headlines do.
- Lead headlines with a strong verb (Build, Get, Start…).
- Speak as "we" when describing what the company does.
- Use precise technical terms; the audience expects them.
- Aim for a grade 10 reading level (Flesch 48).

**Don't**
- Avoid exclamation marks (0 found in 307 lines).
- Don't oversell — superlatives (best, amazing) are rare in this voice.
- Don't use emoji in brand copy.
