Concise

Short sentences averaging 10.1 words.

Evidence-led

Backs claims with specifics — 23% of sentences cite a number.

“Linear powers over 40,000 product teams.”
“Founded in 2019, Linear has become the tool of choice for more than 40,000 companies (including OpenAI, Coinbase, and Ramp) to plan, build, and ship their products.”

Do

  • Keep headlines to about 5 words (site median).
  • Write headlines in sentence case.
  • Speak as "we" when describing what the company does.
  • Back claims with concrete numbers and outcomes.
  • Aim for a grade 7 reading level (Flesch 60).

Don't

  • Don't end headlines with a full stop.
  • Avoid technical jargon; keep language plain.
  • Don't use emoji in brand copy.
