## Voice

Purpose-driven, company-led and evidence-led — serious, formal, respectful and matter-of-fact in tone.

- **Purpose-driven:** Frames the brand around a bigger cause or community. _e.g. “11.2M environmental voters skipped the last election.”_
- **Company-led:** Speaks as "we" — 83% of sentences are about the company. _e.g. “Our Winter ’26 New Arrivals are made for the moments you chase—first chair, fresh trails and far-flung summits.”_
- **Evidence-led:** Backs claims with specifics — 17% of sentences cite a number. _e.g. “Our Winter ’26 New Arrivals are made for the moments you chase—first chair, fresh trails and far-flung summits.”_

**Do**
- Keep headlines to about 3 words (site median).
- Write headlines in Title Case.
- Speak as "we" when describing what the company does.
- Back claims with concrete numbers and outcomes.
- Aim for a grade 11 reading level (Flesch 51).

**Don't**
- Avoid exclamation marks (0 found in 25 lines).
- Avoid technical jargon; keep language plain.
- Don't use emoji in brand copy.

**Mission & beliefs**
- We believe deeply in the urgent, shared responsibility to protect our natural resources.
- As a company whose livelihood depends directly on the conservation of, and access to, America's national forests, we believe both Alternatives 2 and 3 will cause real, lasting harm to our business, the gateway communities in which we operate and the customers we serve.
- We believe it is our responsibility to represent the interests and desires of our community to retain this rule in its entirety.

**Values**
- **Quality:** Build the best product, provide the best service and constantly improve everything we do.
- **Integrity:** Examine our practices openly and honestly, learn from our mistakes and meet our commitments.
- **Environmentalism:** Protect our home planet.
- **Justice:** Be just, equitable and antiracist as a company and in our community.
- **Not bound by convention:** Do it our way.
