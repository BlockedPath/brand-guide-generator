## Voice

Evidence-led, purpose-driven and company-led — serious, formal, respectful and matter-of-fact in tone.

- **Evidence-led:** Backs claims with specifics — 52% of sentences cite a number. _e.g. “The Airbed & Breakfast website launches in time for the Democratic National Convention and gets 80 bookings.”_
- **Purpose-driven:** Frames the brand around a bigger cause or community. _e.g. “Every day, hosts offer unique stays, experiences, and services that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way.”_
- **Company-led:** Speaks as "we" — 28% of sentences are about the company. _e.g. “Airbnb launches Payments, our bespoke payments platform which in 2019 processed about $70 billion in guest and host transactions in over 40 currencies.”_
- **Polished & formal:** Measured, complete sentences at a grade-9.9 reading level. _e.g. “Every day, hosts offer unique stays, experiences, and services that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way.”_

**Do**
- Keep headlines to about 3 words (site median).
- Write headlines in Title Case.
- Speak as "we" when describing what the company does.
- Back claims with concrete numbers and outcomes.
- Aim for a grade 10 reading level (Flesch 52).

**Don't**
- Don't end headlines with a full stop.
- Avoid contractions; keep sentences complete and polished.
- Avoid exclamation marks (0 found in 101 lines).
- Avoid technical jargon; keep language plain.
