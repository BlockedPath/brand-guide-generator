Do

  • Keep headlines to about 3 words (site median).
  • Write headlines in Title Case.
  • Speak as "we" when describing what the company does.
  • Back claims with concrete numbers and outcomes.
  • Aim for a grade 10 reading level (Flesch 52).

Don't

  • Don't end headlines with a full stop.
  • Avoid contractions; keep sentences complete and polished.
  • Avoid exclamation marks (0 found in 101 lines).
  • Avoid technical jargon; keep language plain.
